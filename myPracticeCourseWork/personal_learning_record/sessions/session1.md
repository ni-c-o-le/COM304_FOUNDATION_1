## Personal Notes and research following this session

Book to read - Computer organization and architecture by william stallings 2021

I learned that Git was created by Linus Torvalds, the same person who developed Linux, and it’s essential for version control in collaborative projects.

I researched more about Charles Babbage and Ada Lovelace — Ada is often considered the world’s first computer programmer because she wrote algorithms for Babbage’s Analytical Engine.

I found Alan Turing’s story fascinating, especially his role in decrypting the Enigma machine during WWII and how his concept of the Turing Machine led to modern computing theory.

I looked into the Raspberry Pi website to understand its purpose — a small, affordable computer designed for education, which can be used for programming, networking, and electronics projects.

I also found the evolution of storage interesting — from punch cards and magnetic core memory to modern RAM and ROM technology.
